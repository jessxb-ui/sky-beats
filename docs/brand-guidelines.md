# Sky Beats brand guidelines

## 1. Brand foundation

### Brand idea

Sky Beats is a playful learn-to-DJ app for Jess and Grace. It blends music, flight, confidence and creativity. The experience should feel like a tiny DJ adventure school: warm, fun, encouraging and cool enough for a child without becoming childish.

### Brand promise

Sky Beats helps a parent and child learn DJing together through short, playful music missions that turn practice into a shared creative adventure.

### Tagline

**Learn. Mix. Soar.**

### Personality

- **Playful, not childish:** bright and imaginative, with a polished finish.
- **Confident and kind:** celebrate small wins without making learning intimidating.
- **Creative and energetic:** use music, movement, sound waves, stickers, patches and missions.
- **Bonding-focused:** Jess and Grace learn together; neither is merely the instructor.
- **Cheeky, not chaotic:** light humour is welcome when the next action remains clear.

## 2. Visual identity

### Illustration direction

Use polished soft cartoon realism with:

- clean outlines and warm dimensional shading;
- expressive, friendly faces;
- aviation-inspired jackets, goggles and embroidered patches;
- headphones, decks, notes and sound waves;
- airy backgrounds with colourful graphic accents;
- confident poses that give Jess and Grace equal importance.

Avoid flat corporate illustration, toddler-coded styling, dark nightclub imagery, hyper-technical equipment diagrams and the stereotypical all-black “serious DJ” look.

The canonical character reference is [`jess-and-grace-reference.png`](../assets/images/jess-and-grace-reference.png). The full visual board is [`sky-beats-brand-board.png`](../assets/images/sky-beats-brand-board.png).

### Colour palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Primary use |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Primary | Sky Blue | `#46B6E6` | Primary actions, headers, progress and links |
| Neutral | Cloud White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards and clean surfaces |
| Neutral | Soft Cloud | `#FFF9F3` | Warm page background |
| Neutral | Aviator Navy | `#1E2A44` | Text, icons and strong UI elements |
| Accent | Cherry Red | `#E53935` | Celebration, energetic prompts and Grace accents |
| Accent | Grape Purple | `#7A4DFF` | Rewards, headphones and playful highlights |
| Accent | Mint Teal | `#2EC4B6` | Success, progress and sound waves |
| Accent | Sunset Peach | `#FFB66B` | Stars, patches and achievement moments |

Use Aviator Navy for body text on light surfaces. Reserve Cherry Red for emphasis rather than errors alone. Do not rely on colour as the only way to communicate progress, turns or success. Check actual colour combinations for WCAG 2.2 AA contrast before shipping.

### Colour balance

- 60% Cloud White or Soft Cloud
- 25% Sky Blue and Aviator Navy
- 15% combined accent colours

### Typography

- **Display and headings:** Baloo 2, ExtraBold or Bold.
- **Body and controls:** Nunito, Regular, SemiBold or Bold.
- **Fallbacks:** use rounded system sans-serif fallbacks while fonts load.

Use sentence case for headings and controls. Keep lesson copy short and use large, comfortable type. Avoid all-caps paragraphs, condensed faces and long centred text.

Suggested interface scale:

| Use | Size | Weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hero heading | 40–48 px | ExtraBold |
| Screen heading | 30–36 px | ExtraBold |
| Card heading | 20–24 px | Bold |
| Body | 16–18 px | Regular/SemiBold |
| Control label | 16–18 px | Bold |
| Supporting label | 14 px minimum | SemiBold |

### Logo direction

The preferred mark is **SKY BEATS** in rounded bold lettering inside a cloud-like patch, with wings and a subtle headphones motif. It must work as:

- a full logo with the tagline;
- a compact badge without the tagline;
- an app icon using the `SB` monogram;
- a one-colour completion stamp;
- an embroidered avatar jacket patch.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the `S`. Do not stretch, rotate, recolour outside the palette, add heavy effects or place it over a visually busy crop.

The supplied brand board shows logo direction, not production-ready vector artwork. Create and approve final SVG logo files before public launch.

### Icon system

Use rounded, simple icons with soft corners and a consistent line weight. Appropriate subjects include:

- notes, headphones, decks and sound waves;
- paper planes, clouds, stars, wings and flight paths;
- patches, lightning bolts, loops and sliders;
- achievements, gifts, practice and duo turns.

Icons may use colour, but must remain recognisable in one colour and at 24 px. Pair unfamiliar icons with a text label.

### Graphic motifs

Use dotted flight paths, paper planes, soft clouds, small stars, music notes and colourful waveform fragments. They should add energy around the content, never compete with the instruction or look like tappable controls.

## 3. Interface style

The UI is a learning adventure path:

- lessons are **missions**;
- the progress map is a **flight path**;
- starting is **Take off**;
- finishing a challenge is **Land the mix**;
- achievements are jacket **patches**;
- practice rewards are stickers, gear and gig posters.

Use the metaphor selectively. Plain labels such as “Back”, “Settings” and “Try again” are better when clarity matters.

### Layout principles

- Give every screen one clear primary action.
- Use generous negative space and rounded cards.
- Design primary controls for fingers: at least 44 × 44 px.
- Show whose turn it is with name, avatar, text and colour together.
- Keep essential instructions visible without scrolling during controller practice.
- Prefer one or two short sentences over dense explanations.
- Use motion for feedback, never as the only explanation.

## 4. Rewards and collectables

Rewards should be visual, meaningful and immediately understandable:

- jacket patches;
- headphone colours;
- goggles and sneaker styles;
- controller stickers and skins;
- music-note effects;
- duo name badges;
- personalised gig posters.

Avoid coins, loot-box patterns, public leaderboards, loss aversion and punitive streaks. Completion should create pride and shared memories rather than pressure.

Initial patch set:

| Patch | Earned by |
|---|---|
| Beat Finder | Completing Beat Basics |
| Cue Master | Completing Cue It |
| Bass Pilot | Completing Bass Swap |
| Transition Ace | Completing First Transition |
| Loop Legend | Completing Loop It |
| First Gig | Completing the first 10-minute set |

## 5. Voice and tone

Sky Beats sounds encouraging, cheeky and practical. It gives one useful action at a time and treats mistakes as part of play.

### Write like this

- “Ready for take-off?”
- “Find the beat. Your feet already know.”
- “Grace picks the track. Jess lands the mix.”
- “That transition was spicy. Try it again with less chaos.”
- “Nearly. Land it on beat 1.”
- “Mission complete. You have officially not ruined the song.”
- “New patch unlocked: Beat Finder.”

### Not like this

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| “Complete module 1.” | “Ready for take-off?” |
| “Incorrect.” | “Nearly. Try landing it on beat 1.” |
| “You failed the challenge.” | “That mix got wobbly. Reset and try again.” |
| “User 1 / User 2” | “Jess / Grace” or “Your turn” |
| Long technical definitions | A short instruction followed by practice |

Humour must never embarrass either player. Avoid slang that will date quickly, sarcasm aimed at the learner and jokes about skill, age or family roles.

## 6. Naming conventions

- Product: **Sky Beats**
- Tagline: **Learn. Mix. Soar.**
- Learning units: **Missions**
- Practical activities: **Challenges**
- Free rehearsal: **Practice**
- Completion action: **Land the mix**
- Two-player activity: **Duo challenge**
- Collectable achievement: **Patch**
- Final performance: **First Gig**

## 7. Brand checklist

Before approving a screen or asset, confirm that it:

- feels playful but not babyish;
- gives Jess and Grace equal agency;
- uses approved colour and type roles;
- has a clear primary action and readable copy;
- uses aviation language only when it helps;
- includes accessible labels, contrast and focus states;
- avoids pressure-based rewards;
- looks coherent beside the supplied reference artwork.
